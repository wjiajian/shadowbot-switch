In Copilot, delegate browser investigation to `shadowbot-explorer` through `task`. Include the target, the observation required and the intended handoff. In Explorer, consult the installed `xbot-web-exploration` skill and the descriptions of the available browser tools. Preserve verified selectors and observed page state in the exploration evidence. Pass the finalized evidence back to Copilot before implementing a workflow change.
